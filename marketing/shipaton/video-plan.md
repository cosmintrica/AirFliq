- Use English narration.
- Record the real app and native Finder interactions.
- Keep the final video below two minutes.
- Show trial, purchase, and restore before the end.
- Cancel every AirDrop panel during recording. Do not choose a recipient.
- Do not show personal filenames, email addresses, account details, browser history, or unrelated notifications.
- Upload the final file publicly to YouTube or Vimeo.
